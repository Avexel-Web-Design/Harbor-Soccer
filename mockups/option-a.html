Our programs

Three programs for players from their first season through high school.

Recreational Soccer

Birth years 2018–2021

A fun, developmental program focused on building skills and learning through game-style play. Perfect for kids who are excited or just curious about soccer.

  • Fall and spring seasons
  • Cleats and shin guards required
  • No travel

Fall registration closed

Travel Soccer

Birth years 2011–2017

A player development program with a competitive soccer experience in a supportive, recreational setting.

  • Fall and spring seasons, practices twice weekly
  • Girls play Saturdays, boys play Sundays
  • Travel within Northern Michigan
  • Cleats and shin guards required

Fall registration closed

About Harbor Soccer, Inc.

The mission of Harbor Soccer, Inc. is to promote the game of soccer and enhance each child's skill development. We promote fair play in a fun, safe and recreational environment while encouraging camaraderie, teamwork, and the skills of each individual player.

Harbor Soccer, Inc. is the governing board that organizes and oversees soccer teams in the Harbor Springs area.

Support Harbor Soccer

Each season, Harbor Soccer serves hundreds of young athletes in our community. Maintaining fields, providing equipment, hiring referees, and keeping facilities safe all cost money, and your support goes straight to the players.

Every donation helps keep soccer fun, safe, and affordable for kids in the Harbor Springs area.

Harbor Soccer, Inc. is a 501(c)(3). EIN 38-2887596.

Become a referee

Youth soccer across Michigan is short on referees, and Harbor Soccer feels it. If you love the game, refereeing is a way to stay involved, earn extra income, and make sure our players get good officiating.
